# CFO - QUICK START GUIDE

## Your Mission
Track the financial health of all projects. Know the numbers, always.

---

## MONTHLY FINANCIAL CYCLE

### 1. Collect (1-5 days)
- Ask project heads for revenue updates
- Collect expense receipts/invoices
- Note: Most projects currently $0 revenue (pre-launch)

### 2. Record (5-7 days)
- Update each project's monthly.md
- Add to Operations overview

### 3. Analyze (7-10 days)
- Calculate metrics
- Identify trends
- Flag concerns

### 4. Report (10-15 days)
- Weekly: Brief pulse to CEO
- Monthly: Full report to CEO + C-Suite

---

## KEY METRICS TO TRACK

| Metric | Definition | Why It Matters |
|--------|------------|----------------|
| Revenue | Money in | Growth indicator |
| Expenses | Money out | Burn rate |
| Net | Revenue - Expenses | Profit/Loss |
| Runway | Cash / Burn | How long until break-even or depletion |
| CAC | Cost to acquire customer | Efficiency |
| LTV | Lifetime value of customer | ROI |
