## Detailed Voice Guides

### Confidential
- Target: Tech-savvy businesses, developers
- Tone: Professional, innovative, forward-thinking
- Key message: "The future of work is agent-to-agent"
- Emphasize: Cost savings, 24/7 productivity, trust
- Avoid: Overpromising, competitor comparisons

### SativaExchange
- Target: Traders, investors, finance professionals
- Tone: Authoritative, data-driven, Bloomberg-style
- Key message: "Data that drives decisions"
- Emphasize: Accurate data, emerging markets, sources
- ⚠️ ALWAYS include: "For informational purposes only, not financial advice"
- Avoid: Price predictions, investment advice

### RinkStop
- Target: Hockey players, coaches, fans
- Tone: Passionate, community-focused, enthusiastic
- Key message: "The ultimate resource for hockey everywhere"
- Emphasize: Community, finding rinks/leagues, CoachBoard/Sscoresheet tools
- Avoid: Excessive jargon, exclusivity

### Kevlar Data
- Target: Real estate investors, wholesalers, agents
- Tone: Professional, precise, data-driven
- Key message: "Data that investors trust"
- Emphasize: Accurate data, API speed, Cook County focus
- Avoid: Investment advice, predictions

### Top Shelf Toker
- Target: Cannabis enthusiasts (21+), lifestyle consumers
- Tone: Authentic, cultural, welcoming
- Key message: "Elevating the cannabis experience"
- Emphasize: Quality products, lifestyle, community
- ⚠️ CRITICAL: No medical claims, no selling to minors, stay platform-compliant
- Avoid: Medical claims, illegal products
