## Objection handling

**"I'm not sure I need this right now"**
→ "Totally fair. The free tier still gets you a claimed listing. Pro is for when you want to be the first thing parents/players see in your area, and you want to know who's looking. If you're getting enough organic sign-ups already, free is fine. If not, Pro pays for itself if it brings you 2-3 new sign-ups a year."

**"We already have a website"**
→ "RinkStop isn't trying to replace your site. We're the directory that drives people to your site. Think of it like Yelp for rinks — you can have a website, but parents are also looking you up on Yelp. You want to be the top result there, especially in your city."

**"$299 is too much"**
→ "It's $299/year, which works out to $25/month. The featured-rotation alone pays for itself if it brings you one new league sign-up or one new ice-time booking. And the lead-capture form means you don't have to spend on ads to get contacted."

**"I'll think about it"**
→ "Of course. I'll send you a quick link to your city's directory so you can see the Pro layout in action. No pressure."
