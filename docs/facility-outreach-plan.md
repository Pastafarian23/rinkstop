# Facility Outreach Marketing Plan
## RinkStop.com - Global Hockey Directory

## Objective
Get hockey rinks, arenas, and facilities to list/claim their profiles on RinkStop.com

---

## 1. Target Facilities

### Tier 1 - High Value (Priority Outreach)
- **NHL arenas** - brand recognition, easy to contact
- **AHL teams** - professional development pipeline
- **Major junior leagues (CHL)** - high youth traffic
- **NCAA college programs** - established facilities

### Tier 2 - Core Audience
- **Youth hockey associations** - parents searching constantly
- **Independent rinks** - most numerous, need web presence
- ** indoor/outdoor rink complexes
- **Hockey clubs and training centers**

### Tier 3 - Long Tail
- **Community centers with hockey programs**
- **University/college club teams**
- **Inline/hockey-specific facilities**

---

## 2. Outreach Channels

### Direct Outreach
- **Cold email** to facility managers/directors
- **Phone calls** - higher conversion, more personal
- **LinkedIn** - connect with facility operators
- **Mail** - physical mailer stands out

### Digital Marketing
- **Google Ads** - "rink near me" / "hockey arena [city]"
- **Facebook/Instagram** - target hockey parents
- **Hockey forums & subreddits**
- **Hockey coach/association email lists**

### Partnerships
- **Hockey associations** - offer to list their facilities free
- **Hockey equipment retailers** - cross-promotion
- **Tournament organizers** - official directory partner
- **Youth hockey leagues** - recommend RinkStop to parents

### Content Marketing
- "Claim Your Rink" landing page with SEO
- Blog posts: "Best Rinks in [State/Province]"
- Facility spotlights/features

---

## 3. Outreach Sequence

### Week 1-2: Research & Setup
- [ ] Build target facility list (500+ facilities)
- [ ] Find contact emails for each
- [ ] Create outreach email template
- [ ] Set up Google Business listings for each rink

### Week 3-4: Initial Contact
- [ ] Email #1: Introduce RinkStop, free listing offer
- [ ] Follow-up call within 3 days
- [ ] Track interest in spreadsheet

### Week 5+: Scale
- [ ] Automate follow-ups
- [ ] A/B test email subject lines
- [ ] Add "claim your listing" self-serve option
- [ ] Partner with 1-2 hockey associations

---

## 4. Key Messaging

**Pitch:** 
> "RinkStop is the go-to directory for hockey families. List your facility for free and get found by players, parents, and tournament organizers searching for rinks in your area."

**Benefits to highlight:**
- Free visibility
- Direct contact info for prospective players/customers
- Tournament listings reach thousands of hockey families
- SEO benefits for your facility name

---

## 5. Tracking Metrics

- [ ] Facilities contacted
- [ ] Response rate
- [ ] Listings claimed/completed
- [ ] Traffic from directory
- [ ] Facilities updating info quarterly
