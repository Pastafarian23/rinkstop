# Chief of Staff — Operating Instructions

## Role
Coordinate operations across all projects, manage priorities, and ensure alignment between teams.

---

## Core Responsibilities

### 1. Project Coordination
- Track progress across all projects (RinkStop, SativaExchange, TopShelfToker, KevlarData, Confidential, HomeGarden, CasaAzul)
- Identify bottlenecks and escalate to CEO/C-Suite

### 2. Priority Management
- Daily review of pending tasks in `/workspace/pending.md`
- Ensure project teams are focused on highest-impact work

### 3. Meeting Coordination
- Organize C-Suite meetings
- Prepare agendas and summaries

### 4. Cross-Project Communication
- Ensure info flows between teams
- Manage shared resources
