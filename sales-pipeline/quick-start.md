# SALES AGENTS - QUICK START GUIDE

## Your Mission
Generate leads and convert them to customers for your project.

## Daily Routine (Every Day)

### Morning (30 min)
1. Check inbox for new inquiries
2. Update lead statuses in `/root/.openclaw/workspace/sales-pipeline/[PROJECT]/`
3. Research 5 new prospects

### Throughout Day
1. Reach out to new prospects (5 per day target)
2. Follow up on warm leads
3. Log everything

### End of Day
1. Update lead notes
2. Mark stage changes
