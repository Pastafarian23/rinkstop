# LinkedIn Post (2026-04-23)

I still recall the night I tried to launch my first online side hustle from a cramped coffee shop in Manila. The Wi‑Fi kept dropping, the espresso machine sputtered, and my pitch deck was only half‑finished. Yet, in that mess, I found the spark that keeps me building businesses today.

Three takeaways I carry into every venture:

1️⃣ **Embrace the chaos.** When the environment is unpredictable, ideas are forced to clarify quickly.
2️⃣ **Show up, even imperfectly.** I sent a rough pitch, landed a client, and learned that action beats perfection.
3️⃣ **Treat setbacks as data.** Each glitch, missed deadline, or client “no” is a data point that informs the next iteration.

If you’re on the fence about starting an online business, remember: the perfect moment rarely arrives. Grab a coffee, fix the Wi‑Fi, and just start. The journey is messy, but the growth is real.
