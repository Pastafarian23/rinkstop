When Volatility Hits: A Lesson from the Trading Floor

---

**DRAFT:**

I remember the first time I watched my trading mentor lose a serious amount of money on a corn position.

He didn't flinch.

I asked him later how he could be so calm when most people in that pit looked like they were about to have heart attacks. He just shrugged and said, "I already knew how much I could lose before I walked in. The market was just confirming what I should have already accepted."

That lesson came back to me watching Bitcoin drop below $80,000 today as PPI data showed inflation surging to 6%.

Here's the thing about emerging markets — and crypto is the ultimate emerging market — volatility isn't a bug. It's the feature. The same swings that scare out the paper hands are what create the opportunities for those who stayed in the game long enough to know what they're doing.

My trading mentor spent 30+ years on the CBOT floor. He saw every bubble, every crash, every "this time it's different" moment. His answer was always the same: know your position size, know your exit, and remember — the market doesn't care how you feel.

Today's Bitcoin dip isn't a failure of crypto. It's a test of whether you're treating it like gambling or like a market.
