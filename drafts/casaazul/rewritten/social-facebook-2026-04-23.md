I still remember the first time I walked into Casa Azul’s garden at sunrise. The mist curled around the bamboo arches, and I felt like I was stepping into a secret oasis right in the heart of Cebu. Now, that same garden hosts corporate retreats where teams break out of boardrooms and remember why they love what they do. If you’re planning a workshop, bring your team here, let nature do the brainstorming, and watch ideas blossom just like the hibiscus around us.
