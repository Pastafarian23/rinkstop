## The Cook County Property Tax System: A Primer

Unlike most counties, Cook County uses a triennial reassessment cycle—a property's assessed value is updated every three years, with the exception of Chicago, which reassesses annually. This creates predictable (but sometimes dramatic) swings in tax bills.

The tax formula is straightforward:

**Property Tax = (Assessed Value × Tax Rate) - Exemptions**

However, "straightforward" doesn't mean "simple." The county's equalization factor (a multiplier applied to bring assessed values in line with market reality) often creates confusion. For tax year 2024, the median assessment ratio hovered around 10-11% of market value for residential properties.

## The Exemption Factor

Cook County offers numerous exemptions that can significantly reduce tax liability:

- **Homeowner Exemption (HHE):** Up to $10,000 reduction for owner-occupied properties
- **Senior Citizen Exemption:** Additional $5,000 for homeowners 65+
- **Long-Time Occupant Exemption:** For homeowners in their residence 10+ years
- **Disabled Persons Exemption:** $5,000 reduction

For investors targeting rental properties, these exemptions are largely unavailable—but understanding their impact helps explain why owner-occupied properties may bear a disproportionate tax burden.
