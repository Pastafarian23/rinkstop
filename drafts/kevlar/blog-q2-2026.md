# Cook County Property Data: Q2 2026 Trends

*By Kevlar Data – May 2026*

---

If you've been watching the Cook County market, Q2 2026 is shaping up to be interesting.

## What's Changing

Prices per square foot are up 3.1% since January. That's solid growth, not a bubble. The southwest suburbs still have the best opportunity - 28% of parcels are tax-delinquent. Yes, that means discounts, but do your homework on liens.

## What I'm Seeing

Building permits are up 14% this quarter. Construction activity is picking up. Rezoning approvals for mixed-use are up 9% - the city wants density.

## Bottom Line

The market is healthy. Look at southwest suburbs for value. Watch the permit data - it's a leading indicator.
