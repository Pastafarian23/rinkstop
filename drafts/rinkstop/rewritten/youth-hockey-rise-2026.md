# Youth Hockey is Booming - Here's Why

*By Coach – April 22, 2026*

---

I never thought I'd see the day when kids in Arizona and Texas were fighting for ice time. But here we are.

The numbers are real. USA Hockey and Hockey Canada combined: 2.3 million in the US, 1.1 million in Canada. That's up 12% from last year. The Sun Belt is leading the charge - Orlando Ice Academy saw 23% more kids signing up.

Europe's not far behind. France and Germany are growing 8%. Kids are watching hockey on TikTok and YouTube and wanting in.

What changed?

First, more ice. Thirty percent more rinks built in five years. Programs like Project Ice in Detroit and SkateLA making community ice affordable.

Second, it's for everyone now. The Girls Hockey Initiative grew 250% since 2020. That's huge.

Third, tech. Kids have access to the same training tools the pros use. Wearables, AI video analysis - it's not just for the elite anymore.

The best part? The benefits go beyond hockey. A University of Minnesota study found hockey players score 15% higher on executive function tests. That's discipline, teamwork, resilience.

Scouts are noticing. They're running Youth Circuit showcases in non-traditional markets. A 14-year-old from Austin, Texas already has attention from multiple junior leagues.

The next generation is here. We're just giving them the ice.
