# Facebook Post - 2026-04-23

I still remember the first time I laced up a pair of skates on a thin ice pond in Chicago. The cold bit my fingers, but the thrill of gliding felt like flying. Years later, coaching kids in Cebu, I see that same spark when a shy 8‑year‑old finally nails her first crossover. It reminds me that progress isn’t about dramatic leaps; it’s the tiny, consistent steps we take on the ice that add up to confidence.

If you’re watching your kids or even yourself stumble through drills, celebrate the small wins. A quick pass, a steady stride, a smile after a missed shot – those moments are the real victories. Keep the rink lights on, keep the encouragement flowing, and watch how quickly a community of players grows stronger together.
