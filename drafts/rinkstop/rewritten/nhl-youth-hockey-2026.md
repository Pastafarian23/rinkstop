# NHL Highlights & Youth Hockey Growth - 2026

*By Coach – April 22, 2026*

---

I remember watching hockey as a kid and thinking about how fast the game felt. Now, watching the 2025-26 season, that feeling is back - but faster.

The NHL this season is something else. Eddie Johansson in Seattle? The guy is everywhere - scoring goals, backchecking, just doing it all. 58 points. That's not just talent, that's dedication.

And the goalies - Chicago's Liam O'Connor came out of nowhere. .931 save percentage. Five shutouts in 30 games. That's the kind of story youth players need to hear. Anyone can turn it around.

What's really cool? The power play is evolving. Teams are thinking "position-less" now - whoever's hot, plays. Short-handed goals are up 14%.

But what hits home for me is the youth side. USA Hockey and Hockey Canada reported 1.9 million players this season. That's up 12%. The 6-12 bracket? Waitlists everywhere.

The new training methods make sense. Skill-first academies in Vancouver and Detroit focusing on agility and decision-making. Players testing 27% faster edges after three months. That's not luck - that's proper coaching.

Here's what connects the dots: the NHL's emphasis on two-way play mirrors what we teach kids. Versatility wins. Young players see Johansson doing it all and think "I can do that."

The next generation is ready. We're just giving them the tools.
