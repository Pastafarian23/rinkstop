*By RinkStop – May 2026*

I've seen hockey grow in unexpected places. But Asia? That's the next frontier.

## Why Asia?

Populations in China, Japan, and Southeast Asia are hungry for new sports. Ice rinks are popping up in Manila, Bangkok, and Shanghai.

## The Numbers

Youth hockey programs in Asia grew 31% in 2025. That's not small. That's a movement.

## My Take

If you're a hockey brand, look east. Equipment sponsorships, camp partnerships, content creation - the opportunities are there.
