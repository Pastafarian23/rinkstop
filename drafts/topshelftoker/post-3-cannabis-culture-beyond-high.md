## More Than Just a Substance

Cannabis is often discussed in terms of its effects - the high, the relaxation, the creativity. But there's much more to cannabis culture than getting stoned. Let's explore the rich tapestry of traditions, history, and community that surrounds this remarkable plant.

## A Brief History of Cannabis

### Ancient Origins
- **2700 BCE** - First recorded use in China (Shen Nung pharmacopeia)
- **1500 BCE** - Ayurvedic medicine in India
- **500 BCE** - Hemp rope in Russia
- **200 BCE** - Hemp paper in China
- **100 CE** - Medical use documented in Rome

### Modern History
- **1900s** - Cannabis in tincture medicines (US)
- **1930s** - Marihuana Tax Act (US)
- **1960s** - Counterculture movement
- **1970s** - Controlled Substances Act
- **1996** - California medical cannabis
- **2012** - Colorado and Washington recreational
- **2024** - Germany legalizes recreational

## Traditions and Rituals

### 4/20
The most famous cannabis holiday:
- Origin: Originally a date for cannabis smoking at 4:20 PM
- Now celebrated on April 20th globally
- Events, protests, celebrations worldwide

### 7/10 (710)
Concentrate celebration:
- 710 translates to "OIL" upside down
- Celebrates cannabis concentrates
- July 10th annually

### Cannabis Cups
Competition events:
- Grow competitions
- Product competitions
- Industry networking
- Celebrations of cannabis culture

### Joint Rolling Competitions
Skill-based events:
- Speed rolling
- Creative rolling
- Cannabis cup events globally

## The Language of Cannabis

### Strains
Understanding strain names:

**Sativa:**
- Uplifting, energizing
- Creative, cerebral
- Best for daytime

**Indica:**
- Relaxing, sedating
- Body-focused
- Best for evening/night

**Hybrid:**
- Mix of sativa and indica
- Effects vary by strain

### Common Terms
- **Bud** - Cannabis flower
- **Kief** - Trichome dust
- **Hash** - Concentrated resin
- **Dab** - Concentrate consumption
- **Rosin** - Solventless concentrate
- **Terpenes** - Aromatic compounds
- **Trichomes** - Crystal-like glands

### Slang (for reference)
- Weed, grass, pot, reefer
- Mary Jane, terminator, chronic
- 420, kush, wax, shatter

## The Social Aspect

### Consumption Methods
How people enjoy cannabis:

1. **Smoking** - Joints, blunts, pipes, bongs
2. **Vaporizing** - Flower pens, concentrate vapes
3. **Edibles** - Cannabis-infused foods
4. **Tinctures** - Sublingual drops
5. **Topicals** - lotions, balms
6. **Concentrates** - Dabs, rosin, hash

### The Session
Social consumption:
- Passing a joint or pipe
- Sharing a bong
- Group vaping
- Edibles for sharing

### Cannabis Etiquette
Being a considerate consumer:
- Ask before sharing
- Respect preferences
- Don't pressure others
- Know your limits
- Be discrete in public
