This isn't just another renewable energy project – it's a signal that Uzbekistan's power sector reforms are gaining real traction with global investors.

What makes this particularly interesting from a risk management perspective is how this project is structured. The senior debt financing package represents more than two-thirds of the project's total cost, backed by heavyweight institutions like the Asian Development Bank, Asian Infrastructure Investment Bank, and Standard Chartered Bank. When lenders of this caliber step forward, they're not just betting on wind – they're betting on Uzbekistan's evolving energy sector framework.
