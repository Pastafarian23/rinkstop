Excited to see grassroots sports getting a boost in Cebu! The Provincial Sports Commission just launched a free 10-day summer program offering arnis, basketball, and volleyball for kids in southern and northern Cebu venues.

While this isn't hockey-specific, it shows the growing investment in youth sports development in our region - exactly the kind of foundation that can support hockey growth too. Every basketball court or volleyball net installed is potential future ice time when we build proper facilities.
