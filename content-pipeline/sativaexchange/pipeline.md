## Pipeline Overview

```
RSS Feeds (30+) → Scan Headlines → Select 3-5 Stories → AI Draft (SE Templates)
→ Voice Check (Arnel's Voice) → Review → Publish + Newsletter
```

---

## Daily Workflow

### Morning Scan (20 min)
1. Open feed reader across all 6 niches (Crypto, Green Tech, Energy, Finance, Ag, Cannabis)
2. Flag 3-5 stories with cross-sector potential
3. Identify trading mentor angle (risk management, market psychology, position sizing)

### Midday (30 min)
4. Draft blog post using Template 1 (Blog from RSS Story)
5. Draft social post using Template 2 (Twitter/X)
6. Run voice check — does it sound like Arnel?

### Afternoon (15 min)
7. Add disclaimer for finance content
8. Cross-link to stepdad's risk management funnel where relevant
9. Save draft → post to CEO channel for approval

## Voice Checklist (Arnel's Voice)

Before any SativaExchange content goes live:
- [ ] Written in first person ("I remember...", "My trading mentor would say...")
- [ ] Story or concrete moment in the first 2 sentences
- [ ] Trading mentor perspective included where relevant
- [ ] Sentences are punchy and tight — no fluff
- [ ] Data supports the story, not the other way around
- [ ] Disclaimer included for finance content
- [ ] No corporate jargon ("leverage synergies", "optimized solutions")
- [ ] No price predictions or investment advice
- [ ] Would I say this out loud to a friend?
- [ ] Does it sound like Arnel wrote it?
