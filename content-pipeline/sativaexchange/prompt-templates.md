## TEMPLATE 1: Blog Post from RSS Story

```
Write a blog post for SativaExchange (sativaexchange.com).

SOURCE MATERIAL:
[PASTE NEWS ARTICLE OR RSS STORY HERE]

STRUCTURE: Story Hook → Context → Trading Mentor Insight → Takeaway → Disclaimer
LENGTH: 300-600 words
VOICE: First person, Arnel Larracas. Personal, grounded, earned. 
       Like a friend explaining something they learned the hard way.

INSTRUCTIONS:
1. Open with a specific moment, memory, or bold statement — NOT "In today's..."
2. Summarize the news in 2-3 sentences max — data is supporting evidence, not the main character
3. Connect to Arnel's perspective or trading mentor's wisdom (floor experience, risk management, market psychology)
4. Pull ONE clear, actionable, or reflective takeaway for the reader
5. End with: "For informational purposes only, not financial advice"

TONE: Personal. Like Arnel talking over coffee. Not academic, not corporate, not motivational-speaker-ish.

DO NOT:
- Write in third person about Arnel
- Make price predictions or give investment advice
- Use corporate jargon
- Start with "In today's..." or "In the world of..."
- Write long fluffy paragraphs — keep it punchy
- Forget the disclaimer
```

## TEMPLATE 3: Trading Mentor Insight (Standalone)

```
Write a "Trading Mentor Insight" post for SativaExchange (sativaexchange.com).

TOPIC: [INSERT — e.g., "why position sizing matters in crypto", 
         "what the CBOT floor taught me about risk", 
         "how my trading mentor handles drawdowns"]

STRUCTURE: Mentor's Lesson → Real Example → How It Applies Today → Takeaway
LENGTH: 400-600 words
VOICE: First person, Arnel. This is the GOLD content — the trading mentor angle is SativaExchange's moat.

INSTRUCTIONS:
1. Start with the mentor's actual words or a floor story
2. Explain the lesson in plain language
3. Connect it to a current market situation or news
4. End with a practical takeaway the reader can use
5. Include disclaimer if referencing specific markets

KEY REFERENCE POINTS (use naturally, don't force):
- "Never bet the farm on one position" (position sizing)
- "The best trade is the one you exit before it becomes a bad trade" (stop-losses)
- "The market doesn't care how you feel" (emotional discipline)
- "It's not about being right. It's about staying in the game" (survival)
- Reward should be at least 3x the risk before entering

DO NOT:
- Use the mentor's real name or family relationship
- Make it sound like a textbook — keep it personal and story-driven
- Forget the disclaimer
```
