## DRAFT 1: Blog Post (Template 1)

**Suggested Headline:** Bitcoin Whipsawed Again — Here's What Your Portfolio Needs

---

I remember the first time I watched Bitcoin drop 12% in a single session. It was 2021, I was sitting in my apartment in Cebu, and I felt my stomach drop with it. I'd just put in more than I was comfortable losing — classic mistake.

My trading mentor — a former pit trader on the Chicago Board of Trade who spent 30 years trading corn options — would've had two words for me: *position sizing.*

That lesson hit home again this week. Bitcoin pulled back sharply after Fed Governor Waller hinted at keeping rates higher for longer. The crypto market sold off hard. But here's what caught my eye — it wasn't just crypto that moved.

Energy stocks tracked lower too. Solar ETFs gave back gains from the previous week. Even agricultural futures wobbled as the dollar strengthened on the rate outlook.

This is exactly the kind of cross-market ripple that most people miss when they're staring at one chart. My trading mentor used to call it **"the invisible thread"** — pull on one end, something moves on the other side of the room.

**Here's what this means for emerging market investors:**

When the US tightens, capital flows back to the dollar. Emerging market currencies weaken. That makes dollar-denominated debt more expensive for countries like the Philippines, Brazil, and Indonesia. It trickles into everything — energy projects, ag exports, even your crypto holdings.

The smart move isn't to panic-sell. It's to check your position sizes. Are you overexposed to any one sector? Is your portfolio diversified across enough currencies and asset classes?

I've been asking myself these questions every time there's a market shakeup since I started building SativaExchange. The goal isn't to be right about direction — it's to survive long enough to be in the game when the real opportunities show up.
