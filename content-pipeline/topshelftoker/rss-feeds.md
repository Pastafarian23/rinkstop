## Implementation Notes

### How to Use These Feeds

1. **Set up in Feedly or Inoreader** — both have free tiers that cover 100-150 feeds easily
2. **Daily scan (10-15 min):** Flag 2-3 items worth posting about
3. **Weekly deep dive:** Pick 1-2 items for longer blog/culture posts
4. **Design pairing:** For each content piece, check which TopShelfToker design fits best

### Content-to-Product Mapping

| Content Type | Feed Source | Product Pairing |
|-------------|-------------|-----------------|
| Industry news | Leafly, Marijuana Moment | Statement designs |
| Culture piece | High Times, Dope Magazine | Lifestyle/art designs |
| Design spotlight | Printful blog, Design Milk | Feature specific designs |
| Education | Leafly strains guide | Strain-themed designs |
| Entrepreneurship | Shopify/Printful blog | Brand story |
