## Voice & Tone

### The Voice in 3 Words
**Authentic. Cultural. Welcoming.**

### Core Characteristics

- **Real cannabis culture** — not corporate, not try-hard
- **Lifestyle-first, product-second** — people buy into the culture before they buy the product
- **Inclusive** — veterans and newcomers, all consumption methods
- **Visually driven** — content should pair with strong imagery (Printful designs)
- **Compliance-aware** — never make medical claims, never target minors

### ✅ ALWAYS DO
- Speak to adults (21+) without being exclusionary
- Reference specific products/designs naturally (not like a catalog)
- Share culture: music, art, lifestyle, community
- Include calls-to-action to the store
- Use casual, conversational tone — like talking to a friend at a campsite
- Check compliance before publishing

### ❌ NEVER DO
- Make ANY medical or health claims ("relieves stress", "helps with sleep")
- Use language that implies therapeutic benefits
- Target or appear to target minors
- Use stoner stereotypes (lazy, munchies, etc.)
- Write in stiff/corporate tone
- Reference illegal activity or grey-market sources

## Daily Workflow

### Morning (15 min)
1. Scan cannabis industry feeds (news, culture, trends)
2. Flag 2-3 content opportunities
3. Quick social post ideas

### Midday (20 min)
4. Draft content using prompt templates
5. Compliance self-check (no medical claims, age-appropriate)
6. Pair with Printful design — does a product match the content?

### Afternoon (15 min)
7. Final voice check
8. Schedule posts
9. Update Shopify store links if applicable

## Compliance Checklist (MUST PASS BEFORE PUBLISHING)

- [ ] No medical claims (no "treats", "cures", "relieves", "helps with")
- [ ] No therapeutic language
- [ ] No references to minors or underage use
- [ ] Content is clearly for adult audience (21+)
- [ ] No promotion of illegal activity
- [ ] Platform-specific rules checked (Instagram stricter than Twitter)
- [ ] Claims backed by sourced information where applicable
- [ ] Disclaimer included where appropriate (state-specific)

⚠️ **When in doubt, leave it out.** Compliance > virality.

## Unique TopShelfToker Angles

1. **Design-Driven Content** — Every piece should feature or reference a Printful design. Content drives awareness, design drives sales.
2. **"Elevating the Experience"** — Position the brand as premium cannabis lifestyle, not headshop culture
3. **Print-on-Demand Story** — Lean into the entrepreneurial journey (building a cannabis brand from scratch)
4. **Community Voice** — Feature customer photos, reviews, community stories
5. **Education Over Sales** — Teach people about strains, gear, culture — they'll buy when ready
