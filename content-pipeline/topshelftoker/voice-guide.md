# TopShelfToker Voice Guide — Content Agent Reference
## Cannabis Brand Content — Tone, Rules, Compliance

---

## Brand Identity

**Tagline:** Elevating the cannabis experience
**Store:** topshelftoker.com (Shopify + Printful print-on-demand)
**Audience:** Cannabis enthusiasts 21+, lifestyle consumers, culture lovers

---

## The Voice

### In 3 Words: Authentic. Cultural. Elevated.

Think: A knowledgeable friend who's passionate about cannabis culture and loves great design. Not a dealer. Not a doctor. Not a stoner stereotype. A **tastemaker**.

### Tone Guidelines

- **Conversational** — Like texting a friend who's into the same stuff
- **Knowledgeable** — You know your strains, your gear, your culture
- **Visual** — Always think about what image/design pairs with the content
- **Positive** — Uplifting, welcoming, never preachy or judgmental
- **Culturally fluent** — References music, art, food, lifestyle beyond just cannabis

---

## ✅ ALWAYS DO

1. **Write like a real person** — first person, casual tone
2. **Pair content with product** — reference TopShelfToker designs naturally
3. **Educate and entertain** — give people a reason to follow beyond products
4. **Include a CTA** — visit the store, check a design, join the community
5. **Use visuals** — content should always be paired with strong imagery
6. **Stay current** — reference trends, new drops, cultural moments
7. **Build community** — "we" language, feature customers, create belonging

### ❌ NEVER DO

1. **Make medical/health claims** — EVER. No "relieves stress", "helps sleep", "natural medicine"
2. **Use therapeutic language** — No "wellness benefits", "healing properties"
3. **Target minors** — No language, imagery, or themes that appeal to under-21
4. **Use stoner stereotypes** — No "munchies", "lazy", "forgetful" tropes
5. **Sell hard** — Don't write like a storefront catalog. Build culture first, sales follow
6. **Write corporate** — No "leveraging synergies" or "optimizing the consumer journey"
7. **Reference illegal sources** — No grey market, no illegal dispensaries

---

## Compliance Rules (NON-NEGOTIABLE)

### Medical Claims — Absolute Zero Tolerance
- ❌ "Great for anxiety" → ✅ "A favorite in the community"
- ❌ "Helps with sleep" → ✅ "Perfect for winding down"
- ❌ "Natural pain relief" → ✅ "Beloved by [community] for its [flavor/effect profile]"
- ❌ "Medicinal properties" → ✅ "Known for its [terpene/aroma/flavor] profile"

### Age Compliance
- All content implies 21+ audience
- No cartoon mascots or youth-oriented aesthetics
- Use age-gating language where required by platform

### Platform-Specific Rules
- **Instagram:** No drug paraphernalia in imagery, no explicit references to "getting high"
- **Facebook:** Stricter ad policies — avoid before/after, health claims, lifestyle drug content
- **Twitter/X:** Most lenient — still avoid medical claims
- **TikTok:** No drug use shown — focus on culture, design, lifestyle

## Content Angles That Work

### "Design Spotlight" — Feature a TopShelfToker Design
- Tell the story behind the art
- Connect to cannabis culture
- Link to the product page

### "Culture Piece" — Music, Art, Food, Lifestyle
- Cannabis intersects with everything
- Elevated taste = elevated lifestyle
- Build brand as cultural tastemaker

### "Strain of the Week" — Education
- Strain profiles (flavor, aroma, effects)
- Educational, not medical
- Pair with a relevant design

### "Community Spotlight" — Customer Features
- Customer photos wearing/using products
- Reviews and stories
- Build social proof organically

### "Build Journal" — Entrepreneur Story
- Document building TopShelfToker from scratch
- Print-on-demand learnings
- Entrepreneurial authenticity

## Quality Check Before Publishing

- [ ] Does this sound like someone genuinely into cannabis culture (not corporate)?
- [ ] Is there a visual/design pairing?
- [ ] Zero medical claims? Be honest — re-read carefully.
- [ ] Age-appropriate (21+ implied)?
- [ ] Does it drive toward the store naturally?
- [ ] Culturally relevant and current?
- [ ] Would you actually share this with a friend?
