# TopShelfToker Content Prompt Templates
## Copy-paste into your AI tool — tuned for cannabis brand content

⚠️ **CRITICAL:** Always comply with cannabis advertising regulations. Never make medical claims.

---

## TEMPLATE 1: Culture/Industry News Post

```
Write a social media post for TopShelfToker (topshelftoker.com).

SOURCE MATERIAL:
[PASTE NEWS ARTICLE OR CULTURE TOPIC HERE]

PLATFORM: [Instagram / Twitter / TikTok / Facebook]
MAX LENGTH: [Instagram: 2200 chars / Twitter: 280 chars / TikTok: 80 chars overlay]

TONE: Authentic, cultural, knowledgeable. Like a well-read friend sharing what's cool.
Not preachy, not corporate, not try-hard.

INSTRUCTIONS:
1. Lead with WHY this matters to cannabis culture/community
2. Add a unique angle or perspective — don't just rewrite the article
3. Naturally reference a TopShelfToker design or product ("This 🔥 design 
   captures that same energy..." or "Our latest print was inspired by exactly this vibe...")
4. End with a CTA — visit store, check the design, share their look
5. Include 2-4 relevant hashtags

DO NOT:
- Make ANY medical or health claims
- Use "wellness", "healing", "therapeutic" language
- Reference illegal sources or activity
- Sound like a corporate brand account

COMPLIANCE CHECK: Before posting, verify no medical claims, no minor-targeting language.
```

## TEMPLATE 2: Design Spotlight

```
Write a post showcasing a TopShelfToker design.

DESIGN: [Name/description of the design]
PRODUCT: [T-shirt, hoodie, sticker, etc.]
LINK: [Direct product URL]

TONE: Enthusiastic, artistic, authentic. Talk about the ART, not just the product.

INSTRUCTIONS:
1. Tell the story behind the design — what inspired it? What does it represent?
2. Describe the aesthetic and who it's for
3. Mention the quality (Printful printing, comfort, durability)
4. Include a call-to-action to check it out on the store
5. Suggest styling or use cases

FORMAT IDEAS:
- Instagram: Photo of design + carousel with close-up details
- Twitter: Quick visual + punchy description
- TikTok: "Get ready with me" or "What I'd wear" format featuring the design

DO NOT:
- Write like a product catalog
- Make claims about how it makes you feel (no "this shirt makes you feel...")
- Use generic descriptions ("great quality, cool design")
```

## TEMPLATE 3: Cannabis Education Post

```
Write an educational post about cannabis for TopShelfToker.

TOPIC: [e.g., "terpene profiles", "indica vs sativa myths", "how concentrates are made"]

TONE: Teacher energy. Knowledgeable friend explaining something they're passionate about.
Accessible, not condescending.

LENGTH: 300-500 words (blog) or 100-200 words (social)

INSTRUCTIONS:
1. Hook with something surprising or commonly misunderstood
2. Explain clearly — assume the reader is smart but not an expert
3. Use analogies and real-world comparisons
4. Pair with relevant TopShelfToker design if possible
5. End with engagement question ("What's your experience with this?")

⚠️ COMPLIANCE:
- Do NOT say "helps with" any medical condition
- Do NOT use "medicinal" or "therapeutic"
- Say "known for its flavor profile" not "known for its pain relief"
- Say "popular in the community for" not "used to treat"
- Say "effects include relaxation/creativity/focus" — frame as experience, not treatment
```

## TEMPLATE 4: Build Journal / Entrepreneur Story

```
Write a "building the brand" post for TopShelfToker.

TOPIC: [e.g., "Launching our first print run", "Lessons from Printful", 
         "Why I started a cannabis merch brand"]

TONE: Honest, entrepreneurial, relatable. Vulnerability + hustle.

LENGTH: 400-800 words

INSTRUCTIONS:
1. Start with the real moment — what happened, what you felt
2. Share the lesson or insight
3. Connect it to the bigger vision for the brand
4. End with what's next or ask for community input

WHY THIS WORKS:
- People follow PEOPLE, not brands
- The entrepreneurial journey IS the content
- Print-on-demand has a built-in story arc (design → product → customer)
- This is your unique angle — nobody else has YOUR story
```

## TEMPLATE 5: Community Spotlight

```
Write a community post featuring a TopShelfToker customer or fan.

SOURCE MATERIAL:
[Customer photo, review, story, social media post]

TONE: Grateful, hype, community-building.

INSTRUCTIONS:
1. Feature the person — who they are, what they're about
2. Show how they rep TopShelfToker gear
3. Tag them (with permission)
4. Invite others to share their looks
5. Drive to store for the featured design

FORMAT:
- Instagram: Repost with caption
- Twitter: Quote tweet + comment
- Story: Poll ("Which design should we feature next?")

DO NOT:
- Use someone's image without permission
- Make it feel like a sales pitch
- Forget to engage — reply to every comment on community posts
```
