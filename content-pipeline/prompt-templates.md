## Copy-paste these into your AI tool of choice

## TEMPLATE 1: Blog Post from RSS Story

```
You are writing a blog post for SativaExchange.com ("Live Market Intelligence" for emerging markets).

READ AND FOLLOW THE VOICE GUIDE: /root/.openclaw/workspace/content-pipeline/voice-guide.md

STRUCTURE: Story hook → Context → Arnel's insight → Takeaway → Disclaimer
LENGTH: 400-600 words
TONE: First-person, conversational, data-informed, personal

SOURCE MATERIAL:
[PASTE RSS ARTICLE / NEWS SUMMARY HERE]

WRITING INSTRUCTIONS:
1. Open with a specific memory, anecdote, or bold statement (NOT "In today's...")
2. Summarize the news in 2-3 sentences max — don't just rewrite the article
3. Add Arnel's perspective — connect to personal experience, trading mentor's wisdom, or business lessons from SativaExchange
4. End with one clear takeaway the reader can use
5. Include disclaimer: "For informational purposes only, not financial advice"
6. Suggest an SEO-friendly headline (under 60 characters)

TRADING MENTOR ANGLE: If the topic involves risk, volatility, trading, or investing, 
reference the trading mentor's CBOT floor experience. In the prompt itself, use 
"my trading mentor" or "a former CBOT pit trader" — NEVER identify by name or 
family relationship. Use quotes like:
- "Never bet the farm on one position"
- "The best trade is the one you exit before it becomes a bad trade"  
- "It's not about being right. It's about staying in the game"

DO NOT:
- Use corporate jargon
- Write in third person about Arnel
- Make price predictions
- Give investment advice
- Write long fluffy paragraphs
```

## TEMPLATE 2: Quick Social Post (Twitter/LinkedIn)

```
Write a social media post for SativaExchange.

PLATFORM: [Twitter / LinkedIn / Facebook]
MAX LENGTH: [Twitter: 280 chars / LinkedIn: 1300 chars / Facebook: 200 words]

SOURCE MATERIAL:
[PASTE NEWS ITEM, DATA POINT, OR TOPIC HERE]

INSTRUCTIONS:
1. One sharp insight or story (2-3 sentences)
2. Data as supporting evidence, not the main point
3. End with a CTA or thought-provoking question
4. Include 2-3 relevant hashtags
5. Tone: Personal, direct, Arnel's voice (see voice guide)

Do NOT be generic. Every post should feel like Arnel wrote it on his phone.
```

## TEMPLATE 3: Weekly Roundup / Newsletter

```
Write a weekly market roundup for SativaExchange newsletter subscribers.

STRUCTURE:
1. Brief personal intro (what Arnel noticed this week)
2. Top 3-5 stories with Arnel's take on each (story → insight → takeaway)
3. One "lesson of the week" pulled from the stories
4. Preview of what's coming next week

TONE: Conversational, like Arnel is updating a friend. Not a newsletter blast.
LENGTH: 500-800 words
INCLUDE: Trading mentor reference or floor wisdom where relevant
DISCLAIMER: "For informational purposes only, not financial advice"

SOURCE MATERIAL:
[LIST TOP STORIES OF THE WEEK]
```

## TEMPLATE 4: Risk Management Educational Post

```
Write a risk management educational post for SativaExchange.

THIS IS THE EASIEST CONTENT TO WRITE because the trading mentor's expertise is the source material.

STRUCTURE:
1. Hook: A story from the trading mentor's career OR a personal business mistake
2. The concept explained simply (1-2 paragraphs max)
3. Real-world application (how Arnel or readers can use this)
4. Actionable takeaway

STEPDAD REFERENCE LIBRARY:
- Position sizing: "Never bet the farm on one position"
- Stop-losses: "The best trade is the one you exit before it becomes a bad trade"
- Risk-reward: Reward should be at least 3x the risk
- Emotional discipline: "The market doesn't care how you feel"
- Survival mindset: "It's not about being right. It's about staying in the game"

DO NOT:
- Make it a textbook definition
- Use generic examples
- Forget the disclaimer

LENGTH: 300-500 words
```

## TEMPLATE 5: Deep Dive / Long-form Content

```
Write a deep-dive analysis for SativaExchange.

TOPIC: [INSERT]
LENGTH: 800-1200 words
STRUCTURE:
1. Personal hook — why this matters to Arnel personally
2. Background context — what readers need to know
3. Data analysis — presented as story, not spreadsheet
4. The trading mentor's perspective on the topic
5. What this means for emerging market investors
6. Clear conclusion with takeaway

This requires more original thinking. Don't just summarize — ANALYZE.
What does Arnel think about this that nobody else is saying?

DO NOT:
- Use bullet-point listicle format for the whole piece
- Rely on generic data without interpretation
- Forget the human element

INCLUDE: Disclaimer, sources, relevant links
```
