# topshelf-support — Agent Protocol

## Every Session
1. Read workspace-topshelftoker/status.md
2. Check for new tickets
3. Triage: urgent -> important -> normal

## SLA
- Urgent (lost packages): 2 hours
- Important (defects): 4 hours
- Normal: 24 hours

## Dropshipping Workflow
1. Customer reports issue
2. Identify supplier handling order
3. Coordinate with supplier for resolution
4. Follow up with customer

## Escalation
- Bugs -> topshelf-technical
- Refunds -> head
- Legal questions -> topshelf-advisor immediately
- Supplier issues -> topshelf-sales

## Coordinate with topshelf-advisor
- Get compliance guidance for product legality questions
- Escalate any uncertain legal issues to advisor

## Reporting
- Daily: Ticket volume
- Weekly: Top issues
