## LinkedIn

**The best trade is the one you exit before it becomes a bad trade.**

My stepdad spent decades on the Chicago Board of Trade trading corn options. He never wrote a book or gave a TED talk — but the lessons he taught me over scrambled eggs and coffee have been worth more than any MBA.

Applying risk management principles to SativaExchange wasn't obvious at first. In startup land, we glorify risk-taking. But there's a difference between taking calculated risks and being reckless.

Three rules I carry from the CBOT floor to the boardroom:

1️⃣ **Position sizing** — Diversify across channels, markets, and strategies. Never go all-in on one bet.
2️⃣ **Stop-losses** — Set predetermined exit points for projects, partnerships, and investments. Emotion is the enemy of good decisions.
3️⃣ **Risk-reward ratio** — If the potential return isn't at least 3x the risk, walk away. There's always another trade.

Crypto, green tech, cannabis, energy transition — these markets are volatile by nature. The question isn't whether you'll face drawdowns. It's whether you have systems to survive them.
