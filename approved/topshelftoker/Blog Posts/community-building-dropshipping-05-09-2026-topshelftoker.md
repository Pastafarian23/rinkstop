# How Community Will Make or Break the Next TopShelfToker — And Why I'm Betting on You

*By Arnel – Founder, TopShelfToker*

*May 9, 2026*

I remember the early days of TopShelfToker. It was 2014. I was printing designs in my basement in Chicago, selling at pop-up events on weekends, and building something from nothing. The product was cool, sure. But the real magic? It was the people.

Every weekend, the same faces would show up. They weren't just buying rolling papers and accessories — they were buying into a culture. A community. A vibe. That's what made TopShelfToker different from every other cannabis brand on the shelf.

When we relaunched in 2026, I made a decision that surprised some people: I put community before product.

## Why Community Comes First

Here's the truth about dropshipping — anyone can do it. The barrier to entry is almost zero. You can set up a Shopify store today, connect a supplier tomorrow, and start selling by next week. I've seen it happen a hundred times.

But here's what dropshipping can't give you: a community that shows up for you when things get hard. A group of people who feel ownership over the brand. A base that doesn't just buy — they advocate, they share, they create.

I learned this the hard way. When the pandemic hit and we shut down, I had inventory and a website. What I didn't have was a deep enough connection with the people who loved us. The customers who stuck around were the ones who felt like they were part of something — not just a transaction.

## Community-Driven Design Isn't a Buzzword

When I say TopShelfToker is community-driven, I mean it literally. Before we design a product, we ask what you want. Before we launch a collection, we hear from you.

Here's how it works now:

**You submit ideas.** New designs, product types, colorways, whatever's on your mind.

**The community votes.** We put it to a poll. If the demand is real, we produce it.

**You get the credit.** Ideas that make it to production get tagged to the person who suggested them. Your name, your design, your brand.

I remember those Chicago pop-ups. The energy when someone showed up wearing our gear was electric. They weren't just customers — they were ambassadors. That's what I'm rebuilding. Except this time, I'm giving you a seat at the design table.

## The Dropshipping Advantage

Let me be honest about why dropshipping matters for us. When we had inventory in 2020, I was sitting on thousands of dollars of unsold product in a garage. The pandemic taught me a brutal lesson: inventory is a liability when demand is unpredictable.

Dropshipping flips the model. We don't hold stock. We test demand first, produce second. That means:

- **Lower risk** — We don't bet capital on products nobody wants
- **Faster turnaround** — If a design trends, we ship within days
- **Higher margins** — No warehousing costs eating into profits
- **More creative freedom** — We can experiment without fear of sitting on stock

Every dollar you spend with us right now funds the next phase: our own cannabis product line. That's the endgame. Dropshipping teaches us what you want, builds the cash flow, and gets us ready to manufacture.

## Where This Is Going

I see TopShelfToker becoming more than a store. I see it becoming a platform — a place where cannabis culture lives, designs are born from real demand, and the community drives every decision.

We're 30 days into the relaunch. The Shopify store is live. The designs are rolling in. And the early feedback? It's exactly what I hoped for — people excited to be part of something they helped create.

If you've been with TopShelfToker since the beginning, I owe you more than a thank-you. I owe you proof that the community-first model works. And I'm going to do that one product, one design, one vote at a time.

This time, we're not just selling products. We're building something together.

*– Arnel*
