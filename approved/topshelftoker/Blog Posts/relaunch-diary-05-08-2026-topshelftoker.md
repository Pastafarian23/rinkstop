# From Garage to Global: The TopShelfToker Relaunch Diary

*By Arnel – Founder, TopShelfToker*

*May 8, 2026*

I remember the day I packed up the last box of TopShelfToker inventory from my garage in Chicago. It was 2020. The pandemic had gutted our supply chain, our Shopify sales had flatlined, and I was staring at a warehouse of rolling papers and accessories that nobody was buying. I sat on that garage floor and thought, "Is this it?"

It wasn't. And looking back, that moment — that low point — was actually the beginning of something better.

## The Shutdown That Forced a Reset

Before the pandemic, TopShelfToker was a lifestyle brand. We sold apparel, accessories, gear — the whole vibe. Cannabis culture was booming, and we were riding the wave. But when everything shut down, I had time to think. And thinking turned into rethinking.

I asked myself a question that changed everything: *"What if instead of selling products, we built the brand people actually want to wear and use?"*

That question led me to Cebu. It led me to a new community. And it led me to realize that TopShelfToker wasn't a clothing company — it was a community of people who believe in the culture, the craft, and the creativity behind cannabis.

## The Relaunch Plan

We're not going back to 2019. We're going forward. Here's what the new TopShelfToker looks like:

**Dropshipping-first model.** I learned the hard way that inventory is a liability when you can't predict demand. Our new Shopify store runs lean — dropshipping lets us test products, respond to trends, and keep costs low. If something sells, we scale. If it doesn't, we pivot. No garage full of unsold hoodies.

**Community-driven design.** Before I design a single product, I want to hear from you. What do you want to see? What fits your lifestyle? We're building TopShelfToker with our community, not just for them. I remember when we used to do pop-up events in Chicago — the energy was electric. I want to capture that online.

**The cannabis brand vision.** Long-term, I want to launch our own cannabis products. Not just accessories — actual flower, edibles, and wellness lines. But I'm starting smart. Revenue from dropshipping funds the R&D for our own line. Every sale gets us closer to that goal.

## Why Now?

The legal landscape has shifted dramatically. We've got 30+ states with adult-use frameworks. Canada is fully legal. The EU is moving toward decriminalization. The gray market is shrinking, and legitimate businesses are thriving. This is the window, and I refuse to miss it.

Plus, I've got something most cannabis startups don't: a community that's been with us since 2014. You've been waiting for TopShelfToker to come back. And we're coming back with purpose.

## What's Next

Our Shopify store is being rebuilt right now. New designs. New products. A new vibe that reflects where we've been and where we're going. I'll be sharing the journey transparently — the wins, the setbacks, the late-night design sessions.

If you've been following since the beginning, thank you. If you're just finding us now, welcome. This is more than a brand. It's a story we're writing together.

I remember sitting on that garage floor in 2020 thinking it was over. Turns out, it was just the setup for the comeback.

Stay tuned. We're just getting started.

*– Arnel*
