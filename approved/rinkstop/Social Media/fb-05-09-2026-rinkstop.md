# Facebook – May 9, 2026 | RinkStop

🏒 **What started with 12 kids and napkin sketches now reaches coaches across three continents.**

When I moved to Cebu in 2021, someone asked me, "Where can I find a hockey team in the Philippines?"

I couldn't answer. There was no place to look. No directory. No central hub. Just scattered Facebook groups and crossed fingers.

So I built one.

Today, RinkStop's Global Hockey Directory is approaching 5,000 listings — teams, players, leagues, and facilities searchable by location, age group, and level. Coaches in Ghana, Chile, and Malaysia found us through the directory and are building programs of their own.

Hockey is a global game. It's time we had a global way to find each other.

Add your team. Share your rink. Be part of something bigger. 🌍
