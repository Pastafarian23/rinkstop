## LinkedIn

**Five years ago, I moved from Chicago to Cebu to grow hockey in the Philippines.**

People thought I was crazy. There was virtually no ice hockey infrastructure here. No leagues, no development programs, no market.

Today, RinkStop supports 200+ youth players, digital coaching tools used across Southeast Asia, and a global hockey directory connecting teams, players, and facilities worldwide.

Here's what coaching in the Philippines taught me about leadership:
→ Start before you're ready — imperfect action beats perfect planning
→ Community over competition — organic growth comes from genuine connection
→ Tools solve real problems — our Scoresheet app was born because humidity destroyed our paper scoresheets
→ Culture matters — we built hockey culture by blending the sport with local Filipino values
→ Your unique background is your advantage, not your limitation

If you're building something in an emerging market, the obstacles are real — but so is the opportunity.
