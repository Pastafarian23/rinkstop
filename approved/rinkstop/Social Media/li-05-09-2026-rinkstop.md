# LinkedIn – May 9, 2026 | RinkStop

**There are 80+ countries with organized hockey. Finding reliable information about any of them? Nearly impossible.**

That's the gap I built RinkStop's Global Hockey Directory to fill.

Five years ago, I moved from Chicago to the Philippines to grow youth hockey. I had 12 kids. No infrastructure. And no way to connect with other coaches or leagues outside of random Facebook groups.

Since then:
→ 200+ youth players locally
→ Coaching tools used in 15+ countries
→ A global directory approaching 5,000 listings

The demand isn't what surprised me. It's the quality — coaches in Ghana building grassroots programs, players in Chile searching for leagues, families relocating and needing to find a rink.

Hockey has always been about community. Technology should make that community findable.

If you're building in sports, emerging markets, or community infrastructure — I'd love to connect.
