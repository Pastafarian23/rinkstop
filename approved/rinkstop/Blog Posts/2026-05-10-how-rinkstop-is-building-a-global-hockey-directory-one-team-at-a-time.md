# How RinkStop Is Building a Global Hockey Directory — One Team at a Time

*By Arnel – Founder, RinkStop*

*May 9, 2026*

I remember the first time someone asked me, "Where can I find a hockey team in the Philippines?" It was 2021. I'd just started coaching youth hockey here in Cebu, and the question caught me off guard. Honestly? I had no idea. There was no central place to look. No database. No directory. Just scattered Facebook groups and word of mouth.

That moment stuck with me. If someone in the Philippines couldn't find hockey resources, what about someone in Kenya? Or Brazil? Or Norway? I realized hockey's biggest problem wasn't the sport itself — it was discoverability.

## The Problem Hockey Has

Hockey is a global sport, but finding information about it is like searching for a needle in a haystack. Teams exist in over 80 countries. Leagues run at every level — youth, amateur, semi-pro, professional. But there's no single place where a player moving to a new city, a coach looking for drills, or a family relocating overseas can go and find what's available.

I've lived on three continents. I've coached in Chicago, trained in Africa, and built a program in the Philippines. Every time I moved, the same thing happened: hours of Googling, dead ends, and relying on luck.

That's why I built RinkStop's Global Hockey Directory.

## What We're Building

RinkStop isn't just a website. It's a living, breathing map of the hockey world.

**Teams.** Every team, from the NHL down to youth rec leagues in Southeast Asia. Searchable by location, level, and age group.

**Players.** A global network where players can create profiles, connect with coaches, and find opportunities — whether that's a pickup game in Manila or a tryout in Toronto.

**Leagues.** From organized competition to informal tournaments. If it exists, we want to list it.

**Facilities.** Rinks, training centers, outdoor pads — wherever the puck drops.

I remember when we first launched the directory. We had maybe 200 entries. Today we're pushing past 5,000. And the growth isn't coming from me — it's coming from coaches, players, and fans who keep adding their communities.

## Why This Matters

When I started coaching youth hockey in Cebu, I had 12 kids. Some of them had never seen ice before. Today, we have over 200 players in our local programs. But I also get messages from coaches in Ghana, Chile, and Malaysia who found us through the directory and are building their own programs.

Hockey has always been about community. The game just makes better sense when people can find each other.

CoachBoard — our digital drill-drawing tool — and Scoresheet — our scoring and stats platform — were born from the same idea. Real problems, real solutions. I drew drill diagrams on napkins because nothing else existed. I built Scoresheet because humidity destroyed our paper stats. Now coaches in 15 countries use these tools.

## What's Next

We're adding video integration to CoachBoard so coaches can share drill libraries across borders. We're expanding Scoresheet to support more leagues and languages. And the directory? We're building toward 50,000 entries by end of year.

I'm not trying to replace any hockey institution. I'm trying to connect them all.

If you coach, play, or love hockey — find us at RinkStop. Add your team. Share your story. Be part of something bigger.

Because hockey belongs to everyone. You just need to know where to look.

*Coach Arnel — Connecting the hockey world, one directory entry at a time.*
