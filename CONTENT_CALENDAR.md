# RinkStop Blog Content Calendar

A living playbook for the RinkStop blog. Pick a topic, open a blank doc, write your ass off. No vague ideas here — every post has a clear angle, a suggested image, and a word count so you know when to stop rambling.

---

## The 3 Content Pillars

### 1. Equipment & Gear
Everything a player needs — and needs to know. Stick tech, skate buying, helmet safety, tape jobs, what to buy vs. what to skip. This is the practical pillar. If it goes on the body or in the bag, it lives here.

### 2. Hockey Lifestyle
Hockey as a way of life. The food, the travel, the fandom, the weird rituals every player recognizes. This is the human side of the sport — the stuff that happens off the ice.

### 3. The Game Itself
X's and O's, skill development, strategy, training, and how leagues actually work. For coaches, players, and die-hards who want to understand the game at a deeper level.

## Weekly Posting Schedule Template

Post three times a week. Pick the day that fits your publishing capacity — what matters is consistency.

| Day | Type | What to Post |
|-----|------|-------------|
| **Monday** | News / Updates | NHL scores, player news, trade buzz, game recaps from the weekend. Short, punchy, opinionated. You're a fan who follows the game — write like one. |
| **Wednesday** | Equipment / Training / Strategy | In-depth gear reviews, training guides, tactical breakdowns. This is the pillar content — longer posts, real substance. Thursday works if Monday pushes your schedule too tight. |
| **Friday** | Lifestyle / Listicle / Humor | Fun, relatable content. Lists, stories, opinions, culture. This is the one you write when you're tired but want to stay in front of your audience. |
