# SativaExchange Content Strategy
## Goal: 3-5 quality posts/day → MSN Contributor + Monetization

---

## The Hybrid System (RSS + AI + Your Voice)

### Daily Workflow (3-5 posts/day)

**Morning Batch (2-3 posts):**
1. Pull top headlines from RSS feeds for each niche
2. Use AI to draft analysis (150-300 words) on 2-3 stories
3. Add your twist: 1-2 paragraphs original insight

**Afternoon Batch (1-2 posts):**
- Monitor real-time data (crypto prices, energy markets)
- Quick AI-assisted commentary on significant movements
- Your stepdad's risk management angle = unique differentiator

## Quality Standards (MSN-Ready)

- **Headlines**: Clear, compelling, SEO-friendly (60 chars max)
- **Structure**: Hook + context + analysis + call-to-action
- **E-E-A-T signals**: Show expertise (stepdad's trading background = gold)
- **Accuracy**: Fact-check figures, cite sources
- **Length**: 300-600 words per post (MSN prefers 400+)

---

## SEO Essentials

1. **Unique headlines** — don't just rehash RSS titles
2. **Original analysis** — AI drafts are skeletons, your insights = flesh
3. **Proper attribution** — link to sources, use canonical URLs
4. **Internal linking** — connect related posts
5. **Categories/tags** — clean taxonomy for each niche
