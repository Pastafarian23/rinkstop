# Arnel's Writing Voice - REFERENCE GUIDE

**Last Updated:** 2026-04-23

This is the authoritative reference for how Arnel writes. Always check this file before rewriting any content.

---

## Core Characteristics

- **Personal & authentic** - First-person, "I remember", "I felt"
- **Direct & conversational** - No fluff, no corporate speak
- **Reflective** - Quotes specific memories and moments
- **Values wisdom** - Focus on lessons, growth, lived experience
- **Encouraging** - Lifts others up, "Stay true to who you are"
- **Short to medium sentences** - Punchy but expressive
- **Genuine** - Real experiences, not performative

---

## Example Passages (Direct from Arnel)

### Example 1 - Hurdles:
> "I remember the day I started building my website. Every time something broke, I felt like it was a hurdle to high to jump. But the reality is that each hurdle was a lesson to bring out the best in me."

### Example 2 - Wealth:
> "10 years ago, I felt on top of the world. Everything was going right and I placed value in building wealth. Nowadays, I see that the real wealth is not monetary, but through lived experience and the legacy we will leave."

### Example 3 - Coach Quote:
> "I remember a coach growing up that told me, 'It's not what you do, it's what people think you do.' That left a great impact on me because it opened my eyes to the perspective of others."

### Example 4 - Advice to Younger Self:
> "Looking back on all of the experiences I've had in life, I'd tell my 20-year-old self that no matter how tough life gets, always push through. Stay true to who you are and everything will fall into place as it should."

---

## Writing Rules

✅ DO:
- Write in first-person
- Keep it conversational
- Include real stories/memories
- Use short, punchy sentences
- Share lessons learned
- Be encouraging

❌ DON'T:
- Use corporate jargon
- Write long, fluffy paragraphs
- Be performative or fake
- Use excessive hashtags
- Write in third-person
