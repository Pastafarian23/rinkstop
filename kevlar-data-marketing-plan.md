## Organic Monetization

### Revenue Streams (Priority Order)
1. **Freemium Searches** - 5 free/month, then $19/mo
2. **Property Lists** - $49/mo for automated lists
3. **API Access** - $99/mo for developers/agents
4. **Premium Reports** - $149/report (comps, analysis)
5. **Consulting Calls** - $200/hr (upsell)

### Free Organic Channels
- **Content SEO** - Target: "Cook County property search", "Chicago off-market properties"
- **YouTube** - Tutorial: "How to find investment properties in Chicago"
- **Podcast Appearances** - Chicago real estate investor podcasts
- **Local Meetups** - Chicago REI meetups, mention Kevlar Data

## Paid Alternatives with ROI

| Channel | Cost | Potential ROI | Notes |
|---------|------|---------------|-------|
| **Google Ads** | $500-2k/mo | 3-5x | High intent - "property search Chicago" |
| **Facebook/IG** | $300-1k/mo | 2-3x | Lookalike audiences of investors |
| **LinkedIn Ads** | $500-1.5k/mo | 2-4x | Agent/broker targeting |
| **Nextdoor Ads** | $200-500/mo | 2-3x | Local Chicago neighborhoods |
| **Reddit Ads** | $200-500/mo | 1.5-2x | r/realestate targeting |

### Recommended Paid Stack
1. **Start:** Google Ads ($500/mo) - highest intent
2. **Scale:** Add Facebook retargeting ($300/mo)
3. **Expand:** LinkedIn for B2B ($500/mo)

### ROI Calculation (Conservative)
- $800/mo spend → 40-80 leads → 4-8 sales @ $49-99/mo = **$200-800/mo return** (0.25-1x)
- After optimization (3-6 months): **3-5x ROI** realistic
