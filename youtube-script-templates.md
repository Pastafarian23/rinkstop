# YouTube Script Templates

Two templates for faceless AI-assisted YouTube content.

---

## 🎬 Finance Video Script Template (SativaExchange)

**[Thumbnail]:** "[Headline] — What Traders Need to Know Today"

**[Intro - 10 sec]:**
"Bitcoin just [moved up/down X%]. Energy stocks are heating up. Here's what matters — and what your money is actually at risk of."

**[Hook - 15 sec]:**
"If you're trading [crypto/energy/ag] right now, there's one thing you're probably missing: risk management. Here's what a former Chicago Board of Trade floor trader tells me about today's market."

**[Breakdown - 45 sec]:**
"[STORY 1 - Main Move]
- What's happening: [简要描述]
- Why it matters: [原因]
- What to watch: [关键价位/指标]

**[Breakdown - 45 sec]:**
"[STORY 2 - Secondary]
- Quick take: [要点]
- The angle nobody's talking about: [独特视角]

**[Risk Management CTA - 20 sec]:**
"Here's the truth: the market doesn't care how smart you are — it cares how well you manage risk. The traders who survive aren't the ones who predict every move right; they're the ones who protect their capital when others are greedy.

If you want a former Chicago Board of Trade floor trader's take on risk management — the kind of insight that used to cost a seat on the floor — check the link in the description. That's where I'll be sharing weekly risk assessments from a 20-year veteran of the pits."

**[Subscribe - 5 sec]:**
"Subscribe for daily market breakdowns. I'll see you tomorrow."

## Production Notes

**Stack (Free/Trial):**
- Scripts: ChatGPT or Claude
- Voice: ElevenLabs free tier
- Footage: Pexels/Pixabay
- Editing: CapCut
- Thumbnails: Canva

**Workflow:**
1. Pick 3 stories per day
2. AI generates 90-sec script per video
3. Grab relevant stock footage
4. ElevenLabs voiceover
5. CapCut assemble → upload

**Monetization:**
- 1K subs → AdSense
- Promote SativaExchange / stepdad's service in descriptions
- Affiliate products in descriptions
