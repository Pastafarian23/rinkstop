# 5 Ways to Find Off-Market Properties in Cook County

The best deals aren't on MLS. They're off-market — and here's how to find them in Cook County.

## 1. Drive for Dollars

Drive neighborhoods, look for distressed properties:
- Vacant buildings
- overgrown lawns
- Code enforcement liens
- Tax delinquency

Then find the owner through county records.

## 2. Public Records Searches

Cook County Assessor data is public. Search for:
- Recent foreclosure filings
- Owner-occupied vs. rental
- Long-held properties (likely motivated sellers)

## 3. Target Probate & Inheritance Properties

Properties changing hands after death = motivated sellers. Check recorder filings for probate cases.

## 4. expired Listings

Properties that didn't sell often come back with motivated sellers. Check old MLS data.

## 5. Direct Mail Campaigns

Targeted mail to distressed property owners works. Use county data to find:
- Free clear-title properties
- High-equity homes
- Absent landlords
