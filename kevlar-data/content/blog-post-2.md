# Understanding Cook County Property Records: A Complete Guide

Every investor needs to read county records. Here's how to use them.

## What You Can Find

### Owner Information
- Mailing address (often different from property address)
- Phone numbers (sometimes)
- Entity ownership (LLCs, trusts)

### Property Details
- Square footage
- Year built
- Last sale price & date
- Assessed value
- Tax history

### Liens & Encumbrances
- Mortgages
- Tax liens
- Judgments
- HOA debts

## Where to Find It

### Cook County Assessor
- Property characteristics
- Assessed values
- Exemptions

### Cook County Recorder
- Deeds, mortgages
- Plat maps
- Power of attorney

### Cook County Clerk
- Tax status
- Parcel numbers

---

## Why This Matters

Without this data, you're flying blind. With it, you can:
- Skip properties with hidden liens
- Find motivated sellers
- Negotiate from strength
