# Sub‑Market Spotlight: Lakeview & Naperville, Cook County

*Published: Draft*

## Overview
Lakeview (North‑Side Chicago) and Naperville (Western Suburb) represent two contrasting but complementary sub‑markets within Cook County. Both have attracted investor attention due to solid demand drivers, distinct demographics, and differing risk‑return profiles.

## Lakeview – Urban Core
| Metric | 2024 | 2025 YTD | YoY |
|--------|------|----------|-----|
| Median Condo Price | $310,000 | $322,000 | +3.9% |
| Avg. Rent (2‑bed) | $1,850/mo | $1,910/mo | +3.2% |
| Vacancy Rate | 4.2% | 4.0% | -0.2 pts |
| Days on Market (sale) | 38 days | 35 days | -3 days |

### Drivers
- Proximity to Lake Michigan, nightlife, and CTA Red/Blue Lines.
- Strong student population (DePaul University) fueling rental demand.
- Limited new supply due to zoning constraints, supporting price appreciation.

### Investor Angle
- **Buy‑and‑Hold**: High rent growth, low vacancy – ideal for cash‑flow.
- **Short‑Term Rentals**: Via platforms like Airbnb (subject to local regulations) can boost yields to 8‑9%.

## Naperville – Suburban Stronghold
| Metric | 2024 | 2025 YTD | YoY |
|--------|------|----------|-----|
| Median Home Price | $420,000 | $438,000 | +4.3% |
| Avg. Rent (3‑bed) | $2,200/mo | $2,300/mo | +4.5% |
| Vacancy Rate | 3.8% | 3.6% | -0.2 pts |
| Days on Market (sale) | 55 days | 52 days | -3 days |

### Drivers
- Top‑rated schools and family‑friendly amenities.
- Strong commuter base to Chicago via Metra (approximately 30‑minute commute).
- Ongoing infrastructure upgrades (highway expansions, broadband).

### Investor Angle
- **Single‑Family Rental (SFR)**: Low vacancy, stable long‑term tenants.
- **Value‑Add**: Older homes (pre‑2000) can be renovated for 6‑7% yields.

## Comparative Summary
| Factor | Lakeview | Naperville |
|--------|----------|------------|
| Typical Yield | 5.8% – 6.5% | 5.5% – 6.2% |
| Growth Outlook | 4–5% price YoY | 4–5% price YoY |
| Risk Profile | Medium (urban regulation) | Low (suburban stability) |

## Conclusion
Balancing a portfolio with both an urban core asset like Lakeview and a suburban anchor like Naperville can deliver diversified returns. Investors should calibrate exposure based on tolerance for regulation (Lakeview) versus growth stability (Naperville).
