## 2026 Stanley Cup Playoffs: Second Round Recap

The 2026 Stanley Cup Playoffs delivered intense action through May, with several series going down to the wire. Here's what happened in the Second Round.

### Western Conference: Avalanche Advance, Golden Knights on the Brink

**Colorado Avalanche defeat Minnesota Wild 4-1**

The top-seeded Avalanche dominated the Wild in a 5-game series that saw Colorado flex its offensive muscle. Minnesota managed only one win in Game 3, while Colorado's balanced attack proved too much for the Wild defense to handle.

**Vegas Golden Knights lead Anaheim Ducks 3-2** *(Game 6 pending)*

This series has been the highlight of the Second Round. After splitting the first four games, Vegas took a 3-2 lead with a dramatic overtime victory in Game 5. Anaheim has shown resilience throughout the series, with several one-goal games. The Ducks will look to force a Game 7 when the series resumes.

### Eastern Conference: Sabres Win Thrilling Series

**Buffalo Sabres defeat Montreal Canadiens 4-3**

The most competitive series of the Second Round went the distance. After 7 hard-fought games, Buffalo's home-ice advantage proved decisive in the clincher. Both teams traded blows throughout the series, with momentum shifting multiple times. The Sabres' depth and goaltending made the difference in the crucial moments.

**Conference Finals Preview**

With the Second Round complete, the stage is set for the Conference Finals:

- **Eastern Conference Final:** Buffalo Sabres vs. Carolina Hurricanes (Sabres have home-ice)
- **Western Conference Final:** Colorado Avalanche vs. Vegas Golden Knights (Avalanche have home-ice)

### What This Means for the Stanley Cup Race

Colorado enters the Conference Finals as the clear favorite, having dispatched Minnesota with relative ease. The Avalanche's high-powered offense and solid goaltending make them tough to beat in a seven-game series.

Buffalo's path to the Finals now goes through Carolina. The Hurricanes defeated Philadelphia in their Second Round matchup, setting up an intriguing battle between two teams with different styles.

The Vegas-Anaheim series showcased the depth of Western Conference hockey, with the Ducks pushing the Golden Knights to the brink despite being the lower seed.
