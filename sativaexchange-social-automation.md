## 🤖 AI Prompt Template

Use this with Claude or ChatGPT to generate posts:

### Twitter Post Generator

```
Write a Twitter post about [TOPIC] in under 280 words.
Tone: Professional but conversational
Include:
- 1 key insight or market take
- 1 question at the end to drive engagement
- 2-3 relevant hashtags (e.g., #Crypto, #Trading, #Energy)
Avoid: Hype, jargon, overly complex sentences
```

### LinkedIn Post Generator

```
Write a LinkedIn post about [TOPIC] in 150-300 words.
Tone: Professional, authoritative but approachable
Include:
- A hook that grabs attention in first line
- 1 key insight about [topic]
- Personal angle or experience
- Clear call-to-action or question
- Relevant hashtags at the end
```

### Reddit Post Generator

```
Write a Reddit post for r/[SUBREDDIT] about [TOPIC].
Tone: Helpful, not salesy
Include:
- Context for non-experts
- 1 actionable insight
- Engagement hook (e.g., "What's your take?")
```
