## Types of Ice Rinks You Might Find

### Indoor Rinks
Most common in urban areas. Great for year-round skating with climate-controlled environments.

### Outdoor Rinks
Often seasonal (winter-only) but offer a classic "pond hockey" vibe. Many communities set up temporary rinks in parks.

### Multi-Purpose Arenas
Host hockey games, figure skating competitions, concerts, and public sessions. Usually have the best amenities.

### Community Centers
Smaller, local rinks often run by parks and recreation departments. Great for beginners and casual skaters.
