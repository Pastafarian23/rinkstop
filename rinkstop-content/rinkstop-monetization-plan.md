# RinkStop Monetization Strategy

**Date:** April 2026

---

## Quick Wins for Revenue

These 3 methods can be implemented fast with minimal overhead:

---

## Method 1: Featured Rink Placements

**What:** Premium visibility spots on directory pages

**How to implement:**
- Add "Featured" badge to any rink/team profile
- Display at top of search results (first 3 positions)
- Rotate weekly or monthly

**Pricing ideas:**
- $29/month per rink (local exposure)
- $99/month for "Rink of the Week" hero spot
- Bundle with Method 2 for combo deals

**Time to implement:** 1-2 days (add badge + sorting logic)

---

## Method 2: Lead Generation for Service Providers

**What:** Connect rinks with vendors (ice resurfacing, Zamboni maintenance, equipment suppliers, insurance)

**How to implement:**
- Create "Partner" directory category
- Vendors pay to be in directory
- Or: simple contact form where rinks request quotes → Vendors pay per lead

**Pricing ideas:**
- $49/month for vendor directory listing
- $25-50 per qualified lead passed to vendor
- Bundle with ice rink associations/partnerships

**Time to implement:** 3-5 days (vendor portal + lead capture form)

---

## Method 3: Banner Advertising (AdSterra ready)

**What:** Display ads from hockey-related brands

**How to implement:**
- Sidebar + header banner slots
- Use AdSterra network (already planned for SativaExchange)
- Target by page (rink pages vs. team pages)

**Pricing ideas:**
- $99/month for 300x250 sidebar
- $199/month for 728x90 leaderboard
- $499/month for homepage takeover
- CPC option: $0.25-0.50 per click

**Time to implement:** 1-2 days (AdSterra integration)
