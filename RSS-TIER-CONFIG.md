## Content Priority Rules

1. **Tier 1 feeds → primary content engine.** 3-5 posts/week minimum
2. **Tier 2 feeds → international flavor.** 1-2 posts/week. Keep globally minded audiences engaged
3. **Tier 3 feeds → Local Scenes features.** 1-2 posts/month. Human interest, growth stories, global hockey diversity
4. **Philippines and Africa are NOT excluded** — they're in tier 3 and get 1-2 Local Scenes features per month
